## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, tactile sensitivity, and deep humanity of a personal health journal interwoven with the precision of contemporary medical continuity tools. It departs from cold, clinical sterility and transactional SaaS interfaces, presenting an atmosphere that feels restorative, grounded, and literary.

### Brand Personality & Core Audience
- **Tone:** Empathetic, literary, calm, deliberate, reassuring.
- **Audience:** Patients navigating chronic conditions or holistic continuity programs, alongside clinicians and caregivers who value continuity, narrative clinical context, and thoughtful care reflection.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, presence, cognitive clarity, and relief from medical fatigue. The interface feels like a well-kept medical notebook bound in fine linen rather than an impersonal institutional dashboard.

### Design Movement: Tactile Editorial Minimalist
The system bridges high-end editorial publishing and hand-crafted tactile minimalism:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation:** Soft parchment tones, quiet charcoal ink text, and tactile layered stationery.
- **Humanist Annotations:** Organic margin notes, hand-drawn indicator arrows, and bespoke underlines that mimic a clinician or patient thoughtfully writing in a margin.
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Expansive whitespace, quiet typographic contrast, and gentle deckled separations instead of rigid drop shadows or sterile dividers.

## Colors

The color palette draws entirely from natural pigments, sun-bleached linen, warm inks, and mineral tones. In strict accordance with the brand identity, green primary and secondary tones are entirely omitted.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#8D4B38` - Deep Terracotta Umber):** Applied to focused interactions, primary actions, active timeline nodes, and key clinical milestones.
- **Secondary (`#4A5568` - Muted Slate):** Used for clinical data points, secondary navigational structures, technical metadata, and observational badges.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#F4DDD4` - Muted Peach):** Used for patient voice notes, highlighting, interactive hover states, and warm alert backgrounds.
- **Neutral Primary (`#2C2825` - Deep Warm Sepia/Charcoal):** Applied to headlines, primary reading text, and crisp 1px borders.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#5C5650` - Weathered Charcoal):** Applied to secondary narrative, supporting captions, and metadata.

### Paper & Surface Scales
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F4` (Warm Ivory)
- **Layer 1 Surface (Cards, Journal Panels):** `#FFFDF9` (Pure Off-White Paper)
- **Layer 2 Recessed Surface (Inputs, Groupings):** `#F7F4EC` (Cream)
- **Layer 3 Structural Framing & Rulers:** `#EFECE2` (Light Linen Beige)

### Contextual Washes
- **Reflective Notes:** `#E8E4F0` (Soft Lavender Wash) for emotional wellbeing, sleep observations, and reflective journaling.
- **Clinical Vitality:** `#DEE6EC` (Pale Dusty Blue Wash) for objective physiological metrics, lab imports, and scheduling tokens.

## Typography

The typographical structure is arranged in a deliberate three-tier hierarchy:
1. **Editorial Serif (`Playfair Display`):** Carries historical weight, clinical authority, and literary composure. Used for patient headers, chapter summaries, care-milestone titles, and journal entries.
2. **Humanist Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides high legibility for clinical reading, medication tables, dense care plans, and mobile interaction. 
3. **Cursive Margin Script (`Caveat`):** Serves as an expressive, handwritten annotation layer representing human commentary, personal patient observations, clinician margin notes, and warm editorial callouts.

### Typesetting Guidelines
- **Leading & Rhythm:** Body text maintains an open leading (`1.6x` line height) to prevent cognitive fatigue during extended reading of medical logs.
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Use italicized Playfair Display sparingly for personal quotes, diary timestamps, or reflective pauses.
- **Script Handling:** `Caveat` annotations must remain rotated slightly (-1deg to +2deg) and paired with hand-drawn vector connectors or brackets to maintain the illusion of an authentic handwritten margin entry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a book-spread and editorial broadsheet approach, utilizing an asymmetrical 12-column grid system paired with a dedicated wide margin for peripheral notes and annotations.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 8 primary columns dedicated to clinical records, timelines, and primary care workflows; 4 flanking columns reserved for contextual margin notes, handwritten care reminders, and timeline summaries.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid columns with contextual margin notes folding into expandable inline slips or collapsible side panels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single continuous reading column with handwritten notations floating immediately below relevant cards with subtle left indentations.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Prioritize expansive negative space (`space-xl`) between distinct thematic health episodes, avoiding cluttered interface walls.
- Align content to a strict vertical rhythm based on `0.25rem` (4px) increments, balancing generous breathing space with precise clinical scanability.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, glossy acrylics, and aggressive blur filters. Visual depth is established through physical paper sheet layering, subtle edge contrasts, and light-tinted ambient warmth.

### Principles of Depth
- **Sheet-on-Sheet Elevation:** Elevated cards appear as thick, high-grain parchment resting on a linen desk. The separation between `#FBF9F4` (desk surface) and `#FFFDF9` (sheet surface) provides natural spatial hierarchy.
- **Ambient Paper Drop:** When interactive floating occurs (e.g., hovering cards, open flyouts, modal records), use an ambient, tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -6px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.06), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.04)`.
- **Low-Contrast Ink Outlines:** Containers use a tactile 1px border in `#2C2825` with an opacity between `0.08` and `0.15`, simulating the delicate ruling lines of an archival ledger.
- **Dotted and Dashed Dividers:** Use `1px dashed #5C5650` (opacity `0.25`) for non-urgent structural divisions, medication scheduling rows, and sub-tier timeline segments.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary mixes subtle natural curvature with organic irregularity to capture an artisanal, human touch while maintaining interface stability.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Organic Asymmetry:** High-level cards and journal spreads adopt gentle asymmetrical corners (e.g., `border-radius: 12px 14px 11px 16px`), avoiding machined geometric rigidity.
- **Standard UI Elements:** Interactive inputs, chip filters, and system alerts apply consistent roundedness (`0.5rem` to `1rem`) to keep operational interactions clear and dependable.
- **Handmade Accents:** Separator rules, progress indicators, and highlight backdrops feature slightly varied stroke weights and gentle wavy contours rather than razor-straight vector paths.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Muted pill-form button with `#8D4B38` (Terracotta) background, `#FFFDF9` text, and zero synthetic shine. Hover state shifts gently to `#7A3F2F`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base with a 1px soft charcoal ink border (`rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.2)`), `#2C2825` text, and a `#F7F4EC` wash on hover.
- **Annotation Link / Text Button:** Playfair or Plus Jakarta typography paired with a hand-drawn vector underline in Terracotta or Muted Slate.

### Cards & Journal Spreads
- Built on `#FFFDF9` with a delicate 1px boundary (`rgba(44, 40, 37, 0.09)`).
- Asymmetric corner radiuses (`12px 16px 11px 15px`).
- Internal sections separated by delicate warm-linen rules (`#EFECE2`) or subtle dotted charcoal lines.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Field Canvas:** Subtle `#F7F4EC` fill with an understated bottom-rule or full 1px boundary in `#EFECE2`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#8D4B38` with an ink-drawn sharpness; zero glowing halos.
- **Helper Labels:** Accompanied by optional cursive annotations in `Caveat` (`#5C5650`) to clarify clinician questions in a conversational tone.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Visual Style:** Rounded hand-inked square for checkboxes, soft imperfect circle for radios.
- **Checked State:** Terracotta `#8D4B38` hand-drawn tick glyph (or solid ink dot) replacing generic algorithmic vector checks.

### Chips, Pills & Status Tokens
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), low-saturation badges.
- **Milestone Badge:** Muted Peach `#F4DDD4` background with `#8D4B38` text.
- **Clinical Metric Badge:** Pale Dusty Blue `#DEE6EC` background with `#4A5568` text.
- **Reflective / Mood Badge:** Soft Lavender `#E8E4F0` background with `#5C5650` text.

### Segmented Controls & Minimal Tab Switchers
- Borderless or contained within a recessed `#F7F4EC` pill container.
- Active item uses `#FFFDF9` paper fill, charcoal text, and a soft tinted shadow with an optional organic underline beneath the active label.

### Floating Margin Notes & Sticky Annotations
- Anchored to the right margin on desktop, styled like loose paper slips or sticky notes.
- Uses subtle pastel tinting (`#FFFDF9`, `#F4DDD4`, or `#E8E4F0`) with faint tape or clip graphical accents.
- Uses `Caveat` script for personal narrative reflections and physician bedside observations, connected to core data points via delicate dotted curved lines.
